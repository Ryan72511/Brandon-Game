The live show

SRC 01 — Reality / 2013–2023 / Network Television

Ten years inside the biggest formats on American television — starting in the production office on live awards shows, and working up to producing in the field: pitching cast stories, leading camera crews through pivotal scenes, and sitting with editors in AVID until the cut said what the day actually felt like.

Reality doesn’t write itself. Somebody has to find the story, schedule it, and shoot it before lunch.

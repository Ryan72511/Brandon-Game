# Brandon's Ball Machine

A click-and-watch falling-balls toy. No score, no winning or losing,
no text on screen — just balls, light, gentle music, and paint.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any web browser (just double-click the file).
That's the whole setup — no internet or installs needed.

- **Click anywhere** → a glowing ball drops from that spot and plinks
  down through the pegs.
- Every bounce leaves a **splat of paint**, so the screen slowly
  becomes a glowing painting. It fades very gradually over minutes,
  so there's always room for more color.
- Each peg plays a **soft chime** — high notes at the top, low notes
  at the bottom, all from a pentatonic scale so nothing ever sounds
  harsh or clashing.
- Some clicks release a **rainbow shower** of small balls.
- The three big **breathing bumpers** boop balls away with a deeper
  tone and a ripple, like old-school pinball.
- Balls that settle eventually **melt into the painting** with a
  little sparkle.
- If nobody clicks for a while, the machine drops a ball on its own
  so there's always something to watch.

## Sensory notes

Built to be calm and predictable: no sudden loud sounds, no flashing
or strobing, smooth glows only, and every click gives an immediate,
visible response.

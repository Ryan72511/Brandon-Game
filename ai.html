This is the chapter under construction — and it’s being built by someone who has run actual productions, not demos. The premise is simple: every hour a producer spends re-typing a schedule, hunting a clip, or reformatting a deliverables tracker is an hour not spent on story. AI can give those hours back.

I don’t demo AI. I put it on a call sheet.
